# Men's Health in Houston: Preventive Checkups That Can Save Your Life

Many men only see a doctor when the pain won't let them work anymore. Between the job, the family and everyday responsibilities, their own health always comes last. The problem is that the conditions that most affect Hispanic men — high blood pressure, diabetes, high cholesterol and prostate problems — progress silently for years without clear symptoms.

At Clínica Hispana Cruz 2 we see men from all over Houston every day who arrive with conditions that could have been caught much earlier with a simple checkup. The good news: it's never too late to start taking care of yourself, and it's easier than you think.

## Why Should Men Get Regular Checkups?

The statistics are clear: men visit the doctor far less often than women, so their conditions are detected later, when they are harder to treat.

Prevention works because:

- **High blood pressure doesn't hurt.** It can damage your heart and kidneys for years without you feeling a thing.
- **Diabetes starts silently.** By the time excessive thirst or fatigue appear, your blood sugar has been high for a while.
- **Prostate problems are treatable** when caught early.
- **High cholesterol gives no warning.** The first symptom can be a heart attack.

An annual checkup takes less than an hour and gives you a clear picture of your health.

## Conditions We Treat

### High Blood Pressure, Diabetes and Cholesterol

These are the three most common chronic conditions in adult Hispanic men. We offer [complete chronic condition management](/servicios/condiciones-cronicas) with follow-up, medication adjustment and a nutrition plan.

### Urinary Infections and Urination Problems

Burning, urgency or difficulty urinating can point to a [urinary tract infection](/servicios/infecciones-urinarias) or a prostate problem. Both can be treated.

### Sexual Health

We provide confidential, judgment-free [STD testing and treatment](/servicios/enfermedades-transmision-sexual). Getting tested is an act of responsibility toward yourself and your partner.

### Low Testosterone

Chronic fatigue, loss of muscle mass and low libido can be caused by low testosterone. A blood test gives you the answer.

## Recommended Screenings by Age

| Age | Recommended Screenings |
|------|----------------------|
| 18-29 | Blood pressure, general checkup, and STI testing if sexually active |
| 30-39 | All of the above + yearly glucose, cholesterol and weight check |
| 40-49 | All of the above + PSA (prostate) if family history, and an EKG |
| 50+ | All of the above + yearly PSA, and colon screening as medically advised |

## Warning Signs: Don't Ignore Them

Come to the clinic if you notice any of these symptoms:

- Chest pain or pressure
- Excessive thirst, frequent urination or unexplained weight loss
- Difficulty, burning or blood when urinating
- Constant fatigue that doesn't improve with rest
- Frequent headaches (possible high blood pressure)
- Changes in sexual performance
- Lumps or changes in the testicles

None of these symptoms go away on their own. Catching them early makes all the difference.

## Practical Tips to Protect Your Health

1. **Get a checkup once a year**, even if you feel fine. It's the only way to catch what shows no symptoms.
2. **Know your numbers:** blood pressure, glucose and cholesterol. Write them down and compare them every year.
3. **Move 30 minutes a day.** Walking counts. No gym required.
4. **Cut back on soda and fried food.** They are the main enemies of your heart and blood sugar.
5. **Sleep 7 hours.** Poor sleep raises blood pressure and blood sugar.
6. **Don't wait for pain.** Pain is the last warning sign, not the first.
